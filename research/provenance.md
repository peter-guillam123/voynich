## 3. Wilfrid Voynich

- Born Michał Habdank-Wojnicz, Telšiai (now Lithuania), 31 October 1865 (Old Style). https://www.voynich.nu/curricula.html
- Joined Proletariat 1886; imprisoned; Siberian exile; escaped 12 June 1890; reached London 5 October 1890. Same.
- Bookshop at 1 Soho Square (1898 or 1900, sources differ). Later Piccadilly and New York.
- Married Ethel Boole (daughter of George Boole; author of The Gadfly). Date disputed (1891 or 1902).
- Died 19 March 1930, New York.

## 4. The 2026 singulion paper

- Citation: Colin Layfield and Lisa Fagin Davis, "Singulion Structure and the Voynich Manuscript", Digital Medievalist 19 (2026). https://doi.org/10.4000/16k0a ; https://journals.openedition.org/digitalmedievalist/2331
- "singulion" (verbatim): "A quire of five nested bifolia is called a quinion, four is a quaternion, three is a ternion, two a binion, and a quire with just one bifolium is called a singulion."
- Method: latent semantic analysis of similarity between confoliate, facing and conjoint pages; controls: Aberdeen Bestiary and Speculum Humanae Salvationis.
- Result: in the controls, conjoint and confoliate scores differ significantly (p<0.01); in the Voynich they behave alike (p=0.168). Physical support: each bifolium by a single scribe (two exceptions); drawings cross gutters between non-conjoint pages. Best reordering of the stars section improved facing scores by 20%.
- The paper does not mention the Qur'an. The Qur'an comparison in the Guardian is from Davis's interview, not the paper.

## 6. Commonly claimed but not established

- Rudolf II paid 600 ducats: hearsay in one letter.
- Roger Bacon wrote it: ruled out by the carbon date (Bacon died 1292).
- John Dee or Edward Kelley sold it: speculation from Voynich; the "630 ducats" diary entry records a payment by Dee, not to him. https://www.voynich.nu/extra/sp_history.html
- Found at Villa Mondragone: first stated by Kraus in 1962; contested by Zandbergen 2023.
- Kircher received it in 1666: the letter is dated 1665.
- "Dates from 1404–1438": the parchment does; the ink cannot be carbon dated.
- Kraus donated for tax reasons: no primary source found.

## 7. Where sources disagree

Marci letter year (1665 vs 1666); carbon interval (1404–1438 vs 1404–1435 on one voynich.nu line); shop opening (1898 vs 1900); marriage (1891 vs 1902); purchase year (1911 vs 1912); location of the Jesuit cache; page count (234 vs 204 sides vs 240); foldouts (10 vs 11).
