# Research notes: illustrations and sections

Gathered 7 October 2026 by a research agent, checked against live pages. Items marked [unverified] could not be confirmed on a live page. Forum sources (voynich.ninja, ciphermysteries) are labelled and used only for caveats and debate.

## 1. Section table

Beinecke's catalogue gives six folio ranges; René Zandbergen (voynich.nu) classifies page by page, which puts some herbal pages after f87 and treats the zodiac as its own group.

| Section | Beinecke folio range | Zandbergen's page list | What is drawn | Notable folios |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Herbal / botanical | ff. 1r–66v | Quire 1 (except f1r), Quires 2–7, f57r, f65, f66v, f87, f90, Quire 17 | One, occasionally two, page-filling plants per page with paragraphs of text | f2v, f9v, f33v, f93r |
| Astronomical | ff. 67r–73v (Beinecke lumps zodiac in) | f67–f68 | Sun, moon, star arrangements; Pleiades on f68r3 | f67r1, f68r3 |
| Zodiac | (within 67r–73v) | f70v2, f70v1, f71, f72, f73 | Rings of ~30 figures holding stars round a sign emblem | f70v2 Pisces, f73v Sagittarius |
| Biological / "balneological" | ff. 75r–84v (Quire 13) | Quire 13 | Nude women in green and blue pools, linked by tubes and pipes | f78v/f81r (tubes continue across), f77r, f79v |
| Cosmological | ff. 85r–86v | f67v2, f68v3, f69, f70r1, f70r2, f85r2, rosettes, f86v4, f86v3 | Circular diagrams; the nine-rosette foldout | f85v–f86r rosettes |
| Pharmaceutical | ff. 87r–102v | f88, f89, Quire 19 | Jars with rows of plant parts | f88r, f89r, f99r–f102v |
| Recipes / "stars" | ff. 103r–116v | Quire 20 (f103–f116) plus f58 | Dense text, one star in the margin per short paragraph | f103r–f116r |

Sources: Beinecke pre-1600 catalogue https://pre1600ms.beinecke.library.yale.edu/docs/pre1600.ms408.HTM ; Zandbergen https://www.voynich.nu/illustr.html

## 2. Counts, folios, foldouts, quires, missing leaves

- 102 surviving folios, "originally probably 116 of which 14 are missing"; 18 surviving gatherings, "originally probably 20 of which 2 are completely missing". https://www.voynich.nu/descr.html
- Beinecke: "ff. 102 ... including 5 double-folio, 3 triple-folio, 1 quadruple-folio and 1 sextuple-folio folding leaves." So 10 foldout leaves. https://pre1600ms.beinecke.library.yale.edu/docs/pre1600.ms408.HTM
- Missing folios (Beinecke collation): f12; ff. 59–64 from the centre of quire VIII; f74 followed by stubs; ff. 91, 92, 97, 98; ff. 109–110 central bifolium. Same URL. Zandbergen: f12 and f74 "cut out, stub visible". https://www.voynich.nu/descr.html
- Dimensions: 225 x 160 mm (Beinecke); "about 5 cm thick" (Zandbergen).
- Yale catalogue record: binding "Modern (18th-19th centuries)", decoration "in ink with washes in various shades of green, brown, yellow, blue and red". https://collections.library.yale.edu/manifests/2002046
- Folio numbers "added around or after the time when the codex was first bound in its current form"; quire marks "clearly older than the folio numbers". https://www.voynich.nu/descr.html
- "113 unidentified plant species": from the old Beinecke description; [unverified on a live Yale page].

## 3. The zodiac

- f70v2 Pisces: two fishes; 29 figures in tubs holding stars; "March" in the centre in a different hand; each nymph likely one of the 30 degrees. https://www.voynich.nu/q10/index.html
- f70v1 and f71r Aries: "a skinny sheep or goat eating from a bush"; 15 figures each; April. f71v and f72r1 Taurus, 15 each, May. f72r2 Gemini (clothed couple), June, 30. f72r3 Cancer (two lobster-like creatures), July, 30. f72v3 Leo (maneless lion), August, 30. f72v2 Virgo (clothed woman in a blue gown), September, 30. f72v1 Libra (balance), October, 30. Month names French, different hand. https://www.voynich.nu/q11/index.html
- f73r Scorpio: lizard-like animal; November. f73v Sagittarius: "a man holding a crossbow"; December. f74 missing, "most probably cut out". https://www.voynich.nu/q12/index.html
- Starts with Pisces, "very unusual"; Capricorn and Aquarius probably on the cut-out f74. https://www.voynich.nu/descr.html
- Figures stand in tubs only in Pisces, Aries and Taurus (forum). https://www.voynich.ninja/thread-4536.html
- Crossbow: Sensfelder 2003 dates the crossbow type to the first half of the 14th century; Zandbergen: "most similar to others found in German manuscripts roughly between 1400 and 1500". https://ciphermysteries.com/the-voynich-manuscript/crossbow-article ; https://www.voynich.nu/origin.html
- Zodiac emblems "derive from calendar scenes rather than classical Arateia tradition". https://www.voynich.nu/illustr.html

## 4. The nymphs and the biological section

- Quire 13 has five bifolia, folios 75–84. Pools with green and blue water; f77r a tube with four openings read as the four elements; f79v a woman in the mouth of a fish. https://www.voynich.nu/q13/index.html
- f78v: "The two tubes of water coming out of the tub continue on the other folio that is part of this bifolio (f81r), which is invisible since another bifolio has been bound in between." Same URL.
- Layfield and Davis, "Singulion Structure and the Voynich Manuscript", Digital Medievalist 19 (2026), https://doi.org/10.4000/16k0a : streams on f78v extend across the gutter to f81r; stains and leaf elements cross internal gutters on bifolia 10v|15r, 33v|40r, 58v|65r, 76v|83r; "the bifolia were decorated before being nested and bound". Proposed Q13 order 77|82, 78|81, 75|84, 76|83, 79|80.
- De Balneis Puteolanis resemblance noted by several researchers (forum). https://www.voynich.ninja/printthread.php?tid=2091
- Rampling (Yale 2016 essay, forum summary): predominance of female figures "is not compatible with known alchemical cycles". https://www.voynich.ninja/printthread.php?tid=1745
- Over 500 human figures, about 50 clothed (forum estimates). https://www.voynich.ninja/printthread.php?tid=754
- Scribe 2 wrote the Herbal B pages and the whole of Q13. Davis 2020. https://repository.upenn.edu/mss_sims/vol5/iss1/6

## 5. The rosettes foldout

- Quire XIV is a single sextuple fold-out (Beinecke). Nine circles in a 3 x 3 pattern joined by pathways; "The castle in the upper-right circle and a few other structures clearly have ghibelline / swallow-tail crenellations, an architectural style associated with Northern Italy". https://www.voynich.nu/q14/index.html
- Stolfi: castle with "M-shaped merlets"; "conical pile of scales" near the N rosette (the "volcano"). https://www.ic.unicamp.br/~stolfi/voynich/Notes/040/html/f85v2.htm
- Swallow-tail crenellations "found predominantly in what is now Northern Italy"; Zandbergen's overall reading is "Alpine". https://www.voynich.nu/origin.html
- Caveat (forum): such merlons also appear in 15th-century Germany, Switzerland and Austria. https://www.voynich.ninja/thread-3643.html

## 6. Dating clues from the drawings

- Radiocarbon: "between 1404 and 1438 with 95% probability". https://www.voynich.nu/origin.html
- Fashion (forum): clothing "typical for the period 1400-1430". https://www.voynich.ninja/printthread.php?tid=3903
- Beinecke's highlights page still says "end of the fifteenth or during the sixteenth century", which predates the carbon date. https://beinecke.library.yale.edu/collections/highlights/voynich-manuscript

## 7. Plant identification claims

- O'Neill 1944 (Speculum 19:126): f93r "is quite plainly the common sunflower". https://ic.unicamp.br/~stolfi/voynich/Notes/040/data/f93r ; Zandbergen's table https://www.voynich.nu/extra/herb_oldid.html ; f33v also called a sunflower by Brumbaugh and others.
- f9v: Petersen identifies Viola tricolor with high confidence. https://www.ic.unicamp.br/~stolfi/voynich/Notes/040/html/f9v.htm
- f2v: Zandbergen proposed Nymphaea, hedged. https://ic.unicamp.br/~stolfi/voynich/Notes/040/data/f2v
- Tucker and Talbert, HerbalGram 100 (2013): 37 plants, 6 animals, 1 mineral from New Spain. Rebuttal: Pelling https://ciphermysteries.com/2014/01/21/brand-new-new-world-nahuatl-voynich-manuscript-theory
- "Armadillo" on f80v; critics prefer pangolin or lamb. https://ciphermysteries.com/2009/05/29/is-this-the-way-to-armadillo
- Composites: "the roots of one species having been fastened to the leaves of another" (Kennedy and Churchill 2011 via Wikipedia).

## 8. Materials and making

- Calfskin, identified 2014 by a University of York team (peptide mass fingerprinting; 11 of 12 folios sampled were calf). https://www.voynich.nu/descr.html ; Zyats et al, "Physical Findings", in Clemens (ed.), The Voynich Manuscript (Yale UP, 2016).
- McCrone Associates (2009, released 2011): one iron-gall ink for text and drawings; blue "ground azurite with minor amounts of cuprite"; green "copper resinate" plus copper-chloride compounds; red-brown red ochre; gum binder. https://ciphermysteries.com/2011/06/01/voynich-the-mccrone-report-now-online [primary PDF unreachable]
- No yellow pigment survives, probably faded organic yellow. https://www.voynich.nu/origin.html
- Binding: "s. xviii-xix. Vellum case." (Beinecke). Cover is goat skin (identified 2014); insect holes show the original binding "almost certainly had wooden boards". https://www.voynich.nu/extra/sp_descr.html
- Recipes section: "over 300 short paragraphs, each accompanied by the drawing of a star in the margin"; 324 stars. https://www.voynich.nu/q20/index.html
- Pharmaceutical: "57 rows of herbs", 241 fragments (Petersen). https://www.voynich.nu/illustr.html
