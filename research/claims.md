## Framework theories

1. Hoax by Voynich himself: argued against by the carbon date, the McCrone materials, the codicology and the 17th-century letters. Not strictly impossible (a forger with blank 15th-century vellum); Zandbergen calls that an "unacceptable coincidence". https://mail.voynich.nu/extra/nofake.html
2. Meaningless but not fraudulent: glossolalia (Kennedy and Churchill 2004); mechanisms from Rugg, Schinner, Timm.
3. Artificial language: Friedman, Tiltman. No 15th-century scheme known.
4. Lost or exotic natural language: Bax, Hauer and Kondrak, Ardıç, Cheshire, Tucker. None accepted.
